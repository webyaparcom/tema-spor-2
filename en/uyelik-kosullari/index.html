Membership terms

Termination: A membership can be terminated within the contract term by written notice 30 days in advance; unused full months are refunded.

Freezing: With documentation, a membership can be frozen for up to 60 days a year in case of illness, pregnancy or travel.

Auto-renewal: Monthly packages renew automatically each month; you can cancel by notice 7 days before the renewal date.

Withdrawal: For memberships concluded remotely the withdrawal period is 14 days starting from the day the contract is concluded (Law No. 6502 art. 9).
